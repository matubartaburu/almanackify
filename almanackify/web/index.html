      This page is the interface — the engine runs on your computer. Start it with: python -m almanackify.server

      Read it on your Kindle
      1. Download the EPUB.
      2. Open amazon.com/sendtokindle and drop the file in — or email it as an attachment to your @kindle.com address (your sender must be on Amazon's approved list).
      3. It shows up in your Library in a few minutes. If your Kindle offers “Publisher Font”, leave it on to keep the Garamond look.

      A few ideas from it

      Everything runs on your machine; the only thing fetched is the link you paste.
